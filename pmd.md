# Portfolio Website PRD

> **Theme**: Cybernetic AI & Modern Glassmorphism | **Author**: Alaka Bhargabi Sahu | **Status**: Active

## Purpose
Create a portfolio website that presents my projects, details, and education clearly.

## Audience
Recruiters and visitors who want to know more about my projects, skills, and education. It can also be interviewers.

## Main Goals
- Explain the projects I have worked on.
- Explain who I am quickly.
- Show my strong project.
- Make my skill easy to scale.
- Provide a clear contact action.

## Required Sections
1. **Hero Section**: Quick self-introduction, current degree at GIET University Gunupur (B.Tech AI), Hedera certification badge, dynamic neural particle canvas, and primary CTAs ("Explore Projects", "Get in Touch", "Resume").
2. **About Me**: Narrative background, technical motivations, core competencies, and academic journey in Bhubaneswar, Odisha.
3. **Skills Matrix**: Categorized grid for AI/Machine Learning, Web Engineering, DLT/Hedera, and Core Problem-Solving strengths.
4. **Featured Projects Showcase**: Filterable gallery (`All`, `AI & ML`, `Web3 / Hedera`, `Web Engineering`) spotlighting flagship projects with live links and GitHub repositories.
5. **Education & Certifications**: Dedicated timeline highlighting GIET University Gunupur (B.Tech Artificial Intelligence, 2025–2029) and the Hedera Certified Foundation (HCF) credential.
6. **Contact Section**: One-click email copy (`alakabhargabisahu@gmail.com`), direct LinkedIn profile link, and an interactive message form.
7. **Footer**: Clean copyright and navigation anchors.

## Required Behaviour
- Should work seamlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- Should be easy and intuitive to navigate with a floating sticky navbar.
- Should be ultra-fast with lightweight vanilla assets.
- Project links and live demos should work reliably.
- Easy to understand and digest for recruiters within 30 seconds.
- Should leave a memorable, top-tier impression with rich modern aesthetics.
- The layout should scale gracefully to small screens (360px+).
